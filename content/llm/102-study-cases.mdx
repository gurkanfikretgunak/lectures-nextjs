## Key Learnings

1. **Prompt Engineering Matters**: Well-crafted prompts dramatically improve results
2. **Human-in-the-Loop**: Keep humans for edge cases and oversight
3. **Measure & Iterate**: Track metrics and continuously improve
4. **Guard Rails**: Build safety checks into production systems
5. **Scale Considerations**: Design for high throughput from the start

## Summary

LLMs enable powerful applications across industries when:
- Properly prompted and configured
- Combined with human oversight
- Continuously monitored and improved
- Designed with limitations in mind
