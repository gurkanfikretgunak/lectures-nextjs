# Introduction to RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

Welcome to RAG 101! This lecture introduces the fundamental concepts of Retrieval-Augmented Generation, a technique that allows LLMs to use your private data.

## The Problem: LLM Limitations

Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4 are powerful, but they have two major limitations:
1.  **Cut-off Date**: They only know information up to their training date.
2.  **No Private Knowledge**: They don't know your company's emails, documents, or databases.
3.  **Hallucinations**: They might confidently make up facts when they don't know the answer.

## The Solution: RAG

**Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** is a technique that provides the LLM with the relevant facts *before* it answers a question.

Think of it like an open-book exam:
- **Standard LLM**: Taking an exam from memory.
- **RAG**: Taking an exam with a textbook open to the right page.

## Core Components

### 1. The Knowledge Base (Vector DB)
This is where your data lives. Unlike a traditional SQL database, data here is stored as "vectors" (numbers) representing meaning.

### 2. The Retriever
The system that finds the most relevant information for a user's query.

### 3. The Generator (LLM)
The AI model that takes the retrieved information and the user's question to generate a natural language answer.

## Simple Example

**User Question**: "What is our company's vacation policy?"

**Without RAG**:
> "I don't know about your specific company. Generally, companies offer 2-4 weeks..."

**With RAG**:
1.  **Retrieve**: System searches policy documents and finds: *"Employees get 20 days of PTO per year."*
2.  **Augment**: System creates a prompt:
    *   "Context: Employees get 20 days of PTO per year."
    *   "Question: What is our company's vacation policy?"
3.  **Generate**:
    > "According to the policy, employees get 20 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per year."

## Why Use RAG?

-   **Accuracy**: Reduces hallucinations by grounding answers in facts.
-   **Freshness**: Update the knowledge base without re-training the model.
-   **Security**: Keep sensitive data local; only send relevant snippets to the LLM.
