# RAG 202: Building Production RAG Apps

Welcome to RAG 202! In this final lecture, we tackle the hard part: making RAG work reliably in production.

## 1. The Ingestion Pipeline

Garbage In, Garbage Out. Your RAG system is only as good as your data preparation.

### Chunking Strategies
How do you split your documents?
-   **Character Splitting**: Every 1000 chars. Simple but breaks sentences.
-   **Recursive Splitting**: Tries to keep paragraphs together. Better context.
-   **Semantic Chunking**: Uses an LLM to group related sentences. Best but slow.

### Cleaning
Remove headers, footers, and "noise" (like "Page 1 of 50") before embedding.

## 2. Evaluation (RAGAS)

How do you know if your RAG system is working? You need metrics.
We use a framework called **RAGAS** (RAG Assessment).

### Key Metrics
1.  **Faithfulness**: Is the answer supported by the retrieved context? (Does it hallucinate?)
2.  **Answer Relevance**: Does the answer address the user's question?
3.  **Context Precision**: Is the relevant information ranked highly in the retrieved chunks?

To calculate these, you need a "Golden Dataset" of questions and answers.

## 3. Agentic RAG (Self-Reflection)

The most advanced RAG systems don't just search once. They **think**.

-   **Self-Reflection**: The LLM looks at the retrieved documents and says, "This isn't helpful. I need to search for 'X' instead."
-   **Multi-Hop Reasoning**:
    1.  Q: "Who is the CEO of the company that made the iPhone?"
    2.  Step 1: Search "Who made iPhone?" -> Apple.
    3.  Step 2: Search "CEO of Apple" -> Tim Cook.

## Summary

Building a production RAG system requires:
1.  **Robust Ingestion**: Smart chunking and cleaning.
2.  ** rigorous Evaluation**: Measuring faithfulness and relevance.
3.  **Agentic Behavior**: Allowing the system to self-correct.
